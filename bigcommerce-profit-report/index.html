From revenue to what you keep

A strong sales week can still be a weak profit week if discounts, refunds or shipping costs grew with it. The profit view puts the numbers that move your margin next to each other, for exactly the dates you pick.

  • Revenue for the selected period.
  • Discounts given through coupons and promotions.
  • Shipping charged on orders.
  • Refunds issued.
  • Taxes collected.

Compare any period with the one before

Pick a date range and Orders Analytics compares it with a matching previous range. A 30-day view is compared with the 30 days before it. That makes questions like “did the sale actually pay off?” or “are refunds rising?” quick to answer.

Narrow it down

Combine the profit view with the dashboard filters (customer, location, order status and date) to see profit for one region or one customer group. When an accountant or partner needs the numbers, export them to Excel or CSV.

Frequently asked questions

Which numbers does the profit report include?

Revenue, discounts, shipping, refunds and taxes for the date range you select.

Can I compare this month with last month?

Yes. Any filtered result can be compared with the matching previous date range.

Can I export the report?

Yes. Data can be exported to CSV, XLSX (Excel), JSON or XML, with the columns you choose.
