Frequently asked questions

Can’t find your answer? Contact support.

What is Orders Analytics?

An app for BigCommerce stores that adds a full analytics dashboard for your orders (profits, customers, locations and status), plus public order tracking and team tools like follow-up dates and internal notes.

Can I try it for free?

Yes. Every install starts with a 7-day free trial, and no credit card is required.

Can I add a custom order-tracking system to my store?

Yes. You get a public tracking page, tracking numbers for each order, and customer-visible notes for every order status.

Can my team add private notes and follow-up dates?

Yes. Internal notes are never shown to customers, and you can sort and filter orders by follow-up date.

How fresh is the data?

New orders appear within about 60 seconds.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, from the account page. You keep your plan’s benefits until the end of the paid period.

What happens when the trial ends?

Choose a plan to keep using the dashboard. Without a plan, most features stop and storefront widgets show a small “powered by” link.

What is EnhanceMyStore?

EnhanceMyStore builds software for online stores. Orders Analytics is our analytics and order-tracking app for BigCommerce.
