Four formats, one click

  • Excel (XLSX) for accountants, finance and quick pivots.
  • CSV for spreadsheets, imports and other tools.
  • JSON for developers and integrations.
  • XML for systems that expect it.

Choose the columns you need

Exports don't have to contain every field BigCommerce stores. Pick the columns to include, and Orders Analytics builds the file with just those. Smaller files are easier to share and keep sensitive fields out of reports that don't need them.

Filter first, then export

Because exports come from the analytics dashboard, you can filter by date range, customer, location and order status first. Export only last quarter's refunded orders from one country, or the profit figures for a single month.

How to export your BigCommerce orders

  1. Install Orders Analytics from the BigCommerce App Marketplace and open it from your control panel.
  2. Set your filters: date range, customer, location or status.
  3. Choose the columns, pick CSV, XLSX, JSON or XML, and export.

Frequently asked questions

Can I export BigCommerce orders to Excel?

Yes. Orders Analytics exports to XLSX (Excel), as well as CSV, JSON and XML.

Can I choose which columns are exported?

Yes. You pick the columns, and the export contains only those.

Can I export only some orders?

Yes. Apply filters such as date range, customer, location or order status before exporting.
